# Ghost-job & repost detection

Tier 4 — Breakthrough bets · Status: **todo** · Depends on: [t4-shared-job-index.md](t4-shared-job-index.md)

## What

With a shared index + fleet outcomes, detect what no individual can: postings open 90+ days,
repost cycles, boards/companies that never respond to anyone. Score "posting credibility" and
stop wasting applications on ghosts.

## Why

Uniquely possible because of the multi-user API; ghost jobs are job seekers' #1 silent time
sink. Surfaces on the public jobs page and in campaign pre-filtering.

## Done when

Campaigns deprioritize low-credibility postings and the listing page shows the signal.
