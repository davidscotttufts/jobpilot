# Mobile decision inbox

Tier 4 — Breakthrough bets · Status: **todo** · Depends on: [t2-needs-user-escalation.md](t2-needs-user-escalation.md), [t2-web-push.md](t2-web-push.md)

## What

The escalation queue rendered as one-decision-at-a-time cards on the phone via web push: approve
job, answer salary question, approve outreach draft. The user clears agent questions from
anywhere; the agent never idles waiting.

## Why

The polished UX layer on top of the T2 answer-delivery plumbing — the "wake up to 12
applications and 2 questions" experience.

## Done when

A push tap opens a card, one tap answers, and the parked job resumes.
