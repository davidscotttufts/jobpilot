# The Pilot

The Pilot is JobPilot's autonomous mode. Write your **instructions** once
(goals and limits) and the local agent runs your whole search on its own:
it discovers and scores roles, applies, finds warm intros, networks and follows
up, reviews recruiter replies, and drafts interview replies, prep sheets, and
self-promotion posts. Everything it does lands in a live journal; the few
decisions only you can make arrive as one-tap questions, on your phone if you
enable notifications.

The Pilot supersedes the [campaign modes](/docs/campaigns-and-skills) and runs
all of them for you, but every dashboard action stays available for hands-on
work.

## How a cycle works

The Pilot works in short, self-contained **cycles**:

1. **Sense.** The server compiles a ranked agenda of everything worth doing
   right now: your answers to act on, approved jobs, replies to review,
   searches that are due.
2. **Act.** The agent takes the top item and does exactly that one thing:
   one application, one inbox review, one search.
3. **Record.** The outcome is journaled and the agent sleeps, for seconds when
   work is queued or up to your check interval when idle.

One item per cycle is deliberate. Every result is saved to the server before
the next thing starts, so a crash, reboot, or closed lid never loses work; the
next cycle picks up exactly where the last one stopped. A small orchestrator on
your machine drives the loop, restarts stuck runs, and starts cycles on its own
after a reboot, so no browser tab needs to stay open.

## Starting it

1. Make sure your local agent terminal is running (the dock in the dashboard's
   bottom corner). See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) if it's offline.
2. Open **Pilot** in the workspace, write your instructions, and save.
3. Click **Start**.

Starting pairs your machine with the Pilot once; after that the host runs
cycles on boot, crash, or wake until you stop it.

## Your instructions

One small document steers everything:

- **Goals.** Plain-language direction: roles, priorities, constraints
  ("senior TypeScript remote, at least $150k, by October").
- **Operating limits.** Daily apply cap, minimum match score, check interval.
- **Networking.** Off by default. When on, you set a daily cap, a follow-up
  delay, and per-channel autonomy: email can be **draft**, **review**, or
  **auto**, while LinkedIn is always review.
- **Searches.** The Pilot creates these from your goals and re-runs them on
  demand, soon after a run that turns up new roles and less often once a
  search keeps coming up dry. The Instructions page shows them read-only, with
  each one's recent yield and next check.
- **Platforms.** Where the Pilot drafts self-promotion posts. Posting is
  always review-only.

Soft judgment lives in your goals text; the caps are enforced server-side, so
the Pilot can never exceed them regardless of wording. Edit any time, and
changes take effect on the next cycle.

## What runs on its own vs. what asks you

| Runs on its own | Always asks you |
| --- | --- |
| Applies to matching roles within your daily cap | A 2FA / login code (a one-tap card, ~5-minute window) |
| Sends networking emails you set to auto, within your cap | Salary and screening questions on an application |
| Follows up on networking messages per your autonomy setting | Sending a LinkedIn InMail |
| Reviews recruiter replies in your inbox (proposes moves) | Posting any self-promotion publicly |
| Drafts emails, follow-ups, interview replies, prep sheets, and posts | Parking a board that keeps failing |

Anything in the right column surfaces as a question. Nothing sends, posts, or
changes an application's status without you.

## Questions and push notifications

When the Pilot hits a decision only you can make, it posts a **question**, a
one-decision card under **Needs attention** on the Overview tab, and parks the
related work until you answer. Turn on **Notifications** (Instructions tab, per
device) to get a web push when a question opens, even with the tab closed. A
2FA request self-expires after about five minutes; if you miss it, the Pilot
skips that job cleanly and moves on.

## The journal and morning digest

Every action lands in the **journal** on the Activity tab, a live feed you can
filter by kind or group **by cycle** to see what each cycle did, its outcome,
and how long it took. The Overview tab shows the latest activity plus when the
next cycle will wake. Once a day the last 24 hours roll into a **morning
digest**: applications, replies reviewed, messages sent, questions waiting.
**Export** downloads the full history as a file.

## Interview autonomy

When a recruiter's reply is an interview invite, the Pilot drafts a short
availability reply and posts it as an approval card; it never sends on its
own. It also builds a **prep sheet** (behavioral, technical, system design,
company notes) and attaches it to the application.

## Troubleshooting

- **Host offline.** Start the agent from the dock. The Pilot resumes on its
  own once the host is back.
- **Pilot paused after repeated stuck runs.** The host takes a break to
  protect your machine. Check the dock terminal for what got stuck, then
  start it again.
- **A board keeps failing.** The Pilot probes it once and journals what it
  found, usually an expired login it re-establishes on the spot. Everything
  else keeps running either way.
